## python-sales-analysis

# Objective:
- Improve customer experience by analyzing sales data
- Increase revenue

# Project Activities
- Performed data cleaning and data transformation.
- Conducted Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on sales datasets.
- Analyzed customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and sales trends.
- Created visualizations to identify patterns and support decision-making.

# Key Insights
- Female customers contributed the highest number of purchases and overall sales.
- Customers aged 26–35 represented a significant customer segment.
- Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka generated the highest sales.
- Married women showed strong purchasing behavior.
- IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals were among the top customer groups.
- Food, Clothing, and Electronics were the highest-selling product categories.

# Conclusion
The analysis revealed that married women aged 26–35 from Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation were the most valuable customer segment. These insights can help businesses improve customer targeting, inventory planning, and marketing strategies.
